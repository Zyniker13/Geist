## 1. Summary and Design Pillars

**Hook:** Build clockwork animals gear by gear in a workshop grid, then send them to fight other players' automata in asynchronous arena bouts.

**Summary:** A run is a season in the arena. Each round the player visits the workshop: buy parts from a five-slot shop, place them on a 6×6 chassis grid, and mesh gears so power flows from the mainspring to the limbs. Gear ratios trade speed for torque: a small gear driving a large one turns slowly but hits hard. Then the automaton fights a ghost (a saved build from another player, or from the bundled pool) in a fully automatic, deterministic bout. Win ten bouts before losing five. Archetypes (beetle, hummingbird, bear, scorpion, owl, and octopus) reward different train layouts, and counters keep any single build from dominating.

**Pillars:**

1. **The machine is the puzzle.** Every purchase raises a spatial question: where does it go, and what does it mesh with? If a reviewer can play well without looking at the grid, the pillar fails.
2. **You can see why you won or lost.** Every bout can be replayed at 0.25× with power flow visualized; damage to a gear visibly stops everything downstream.
3. **Tactile craft.** Dragging a gear and feeling teeth engage (haptic plus click) must feel good on its own.
4. **No live opponent, no time pressure.** Workshop rounds are untimed; ghosts are snapshots.
5. **Counters exist for everything.** In sims, no archetype exceeds a 58% win rate against the field at round 10+.

**Aesthetic spec:**

| Element | Spec |
| --- | --- |
| Palette | Brass `#B5893B`, brass highlight `#E8C877`, aged brass `#7A5A26`, mahogany `#4A2418`, velvet crimson `#7D1F2C`, steel `#8E979E`, lamplight `#FFD99A`, shadow `#1A120C`, enamel teal accent `#2F7F7A` |

| SFX | Tooth engagement clicks, ratchet, spring wind, metallic impacts by material, a falling-gear clatter on part destruction |
| Haptics | Transient per tooth engagement when meshing (2–6 ticks depending on gear size); rumble pattern while winding; sharp hit on part destruction |

**Inspirations:** Backpack Battles' asynchronous grid auto-battler, Mechabellum's counter-play, and Balatro's readable score feedback.

## 2. Tech Stack Decision

Per [`_CONVENTIONS.md`](../_shared/_CONVENTIONS.md) §2, with these game-specific choices:

- **Renderer:** SpriteKit with normal-mapped textures and two `SKLightNode`s (warm key lamp, cool fill). The bout scene is a side view of two automata; the workshop is a top-down tray.
- **Power simulation:** graph solved once per build change (and on part destruction during a bout) with integer rational ratios (`numerator/denominator` reduced by GCD) so RPM and torque are exact.
- **Bout tick:** 20 ticks per second, deterministic.
- **Ghost storage:** v1.0 default is a bundled, offline ghost pool generated by CWMAI build bots (≈ 20,000 builds across rounds and records) and refreshed by app updates. - **Audio:** AVAudioEngine; music-box stems time-stretched by `AVAudioUnitVarispeed` according to mainspring tension.

## 4. Core Rules Specification

Copy this section verbatim to `docs/RULES.md` in S0.5.

### 4.1 Run Structure

- **R-1 Run.** A run alternates Workshop and Bout phases. The run ends with a **win** at 10 bout wins or a **loss** at 5 bout losses (`balance.run.winsTarget`, `balance.run.lives`). Max rounds = 14.
- **R-2 Archetype pick.** At run start, the player picks one of three archetypes offered (from six), drawn by the run RNG. The archetype grants a unique starting mainspring and a passive (R-40).
- **R-3 Coins.** The player gains `balance.economy.coinsPerRound` (default 10) coins at the start of each Workshop phase, plus 1 coin per win streak step (max +3). Unspent coins carry over up to 20.

### 4.2 Chassis and Parts

- **R-4 Chassis.** A 6×6 grid. Each part occupies 1–4 cells in a fixed footprint (rotatable in 90° steps). Parts may not overlap or extend off-grid.
- **R-5 Part kinds.** `mainspring` (power source; exactly one per build), `gear` (sizes S=8, M=12, L=16, XL=24 teeth; footprint 1×1, 1×1, 2×2, 2×2), `shaft` (1×N, carries rotation in a straight line without changing ratio), `escapement` (regulates; see R-15), `limb` (actuator: jaw, claw, wing, leg, tail, stinger, beak, tentacle), `armor` (passive HP and damage reduction to adjacent cells), `cam` (converts rotation into periodic pulses for limbs that need it), `flywheel` (stores power; see R-16).
- **R-6 Part levels.** Buying a duplicate of an owned part offers to **merge**: three copies of the same part at level *n* become one at level *n+1* (max 3). Levels scale stats per `parts.json`.
- **R-7 Bench.** Up to 6 unplaced parts may sit on the bench. Bench parts do nothing in bouts.

### 4.3 Power Flow

- **R-8 Driving.** The mainspring drives the part(s) in its **output cells** (one cell edge marked on its footprint) at base speed `S₀` (RPM, integer) and base torque `T₀`, from `parts.json`.
- **R-9 Meshing.** Two gears **mesh** when their footprints share at least one cell edge and neither is a shaft. A gear can mesh with up to 4 neighbors. A shaft **couples** to parts at both ends of its long axis (same speed and torque, no ratio change).
- **R-10 Ratio.** When gear A (teeth `a`) drives gear B (teeth `b`): `speed_B = speed_A × a / b`, `torque_B = torque_A × b / a × η`, where `η` is mesh efficiency (default 95/100 per mesh). Ratios are exact rationals; torque efficiency is applied as `Fixed` and floored to integer at output.
- **R-11 Direction.** Each mesh reverses rotation direction; couplings preserve it.
- **R-12 Conflicts (binding).** If a gear would be driven at two different speeds, or with two different directions, the train **binds**: every part in the connected component stops (speed 0) and the workshop shows a red bind marker on the conflicting mesh. A bound component produces no actions in a bout.
- **R-13 Power split.** When one gear drives several outputs, torque is divided equally among outputs (integer division, remainder to the lowest-ID branch); speed is unaffected.
- **R-14 Limb drive.** A limb is driven when one of its input cells is adjacent to a driven gear or shaft end. Its action interval is `ceil(limb.baseInterval × S_ref / speed)` ticks, where `S_ref = 60`; its hit damage is `floor(limb.baseDamage × torque / T_ref)`, `T_ref = 100`. Each limb has `minTorque`; below it the limb stalls (no actions).
- **R-15 Escapement.** An escapement placed in a train caps downstream speed at its rated speed and adds +20% crit chance to limbs it drives. It also reduces mainspring drain (R-17) by 25% for its branch.
- **R-16 Flywheel.** A flywheel in a train stores up to `capacity` energy; while the mainspring is unwound (R-17), the flywheel keeps its branch running until empty.
- **R-17 Mainspring stamina.** The mainspring has `wind` (energy units). Each limb action drains `limb.cost × (torque / T_ref)` energy (rounded up). At 0 wind, all trains stop except flywheel branches. Winding does not happen mid-bout.

### 4.4 Bouts

- **R-18 Setup.** Two builds face each other in a side-view arena at distance 3 (abstract units). Each build's HP is the sum of part HP. Parts have positions on their own chassis; the **front** is the column facing the opponent (column 5 for the left build, mirrored for the right).
- **R-19 Tick order.** Each tick: (1) advance action timers for every driven, non-stalled limb, in build order (left, right) and part ID order; (2) collect actions whose timer reached its interval; (3) resolve all collected actions **simultaneously** (damage computed from start-of-tick state, applied after); (4) remove destroyed parts and re-solve power graphs for affected builds; (5) apply status effects (R-24); (6) drain stamina; (7) check end.
- **R-20 Reach.** Limbs have reach `melee` (only hits front-column parts) or `ranged` (hits any column). Distance does not change in v1.0 except via `leg` limbs: a build with ≥ 2 driven legs may close to distance 1, giving its melee limbs +25% damage.
- **R-21 Targeting.** A melee hit targets the enemy part in the front column on the attacker's limb row (or the nearest row with a part, ties to the lower row). Ranged limbs target by their `targetPriority`: `gears` (highest teeth count first), `limbs`, `mainspring`, or `armor`.
- **R-22 Armor.** Armor reduces damage to parts in adjacent cells by its `reduction` (flat, min 1 damage) and absorbs hits on itself first if it is in the targeted cell's row and closer to the front.
- **R-23 Destruction cascade.** When a gear or shaft is destroyed, everything downstream loses drive on the same tick (re-solve per step 4). When the mainspring is destroyed, the build is **disabled**.
- **R-24 Status effects.** `jammed` (from stinger: a gear stops for 40 ticks), `sprung` (from claw crit: mainspring loses 10% wind), `rusted` (from tentacle: −20% speed for one branch for 100 ticks). Effects do not stack in duration; reapplication refreshes.
- **R-25 Crits.** Each limb action crits on a roll from the **bout RNG** below `critChance` (base 5%); crits deal ×2 damage and apply the limb's on-crit effect.
- **R-26 End.** A bout ends when a build is disabled or reaches 0 HP (that build loses), or both are inactive (no driven limbs and no wind) for 60 ticks, or at tick 1,200. On timeout or mutual inactivity, the build with the higher remaining HP fraction wins; exact ties go to the ghost (defender advantage keeps ties from rewarding stalling).
- **R-27 Bout RNG.** Seed = `SplitMix64(runSeed ^ round ^ ghostID)`. The player's build and the ghost build are fully known before the bout; outcomes are deterministic and replayable.

### 4.5 Shop and Economy

- **R-28 Shop offers.** Five offers are drawn each Workshop phase from the current season's pool weighted by rarity and round (`shop.json`).
- **R-29 Prices.** Common 3, uncommon 5, rare 7, legendary 10 coins. Selling a part refunds half its price rounded down (merged parts refund half the total).
- **R-30 Reroll.** Rerolling costs 1 coin the first time per round, +1 each subsequent reroll.
- **R-31 Freeze.** The player may freeze up to 2 offers so they persist into the next round.
- **R-32 Archetype affinity.** Offers have a 30% chance per slot to be drawn from the chosen archetype's affinity list instead of the general pool.

### 4.6 Ghost Matching

- **R-33 Ghost pool.** A ghost is a snapshot `(build, round, wins, losses, archetype, contentVersion)`. Opponents are drawn from ghosts with the same round and |wins − w| ≤ 1, |losses − l| ≤ 1 (widening in steps if fewer than 20 candidates), using the run RNG.
- **R-34 Ghost validity.** A ghost whose content version is older than the current version is migrated through `GhostMigration`; if migration is impossible (removed part), the part is replaced by its listed successor or the ghost is skipped.
- **R-35 Own snapshots.** After each Workshop phase the player's build is snapshotted locally (for history, and for upload if online ghosts are enabled).

### 4.7 Archetypes

- **R-40 Passives.** Each archetype has one passive defined in `archetypes.json`, for example: Beetle (`armor` reduction +1), Hummingbird (+15% speed for wings; mainspring wind −10%), Bear (+15% torque on claws), Scorpion (stingers jam for 60 ticks), Owl (escapements also give +10% damage), Octopus (+1 bench slot and power split remainder to every branch).

### 4.8 Edge Cases

- **R-41** A mainspring with no output connection still counts as a part (HP) but drives nothing.
- **R-42** Placing a part that would bind a train is allowed in the workshop (the player sees the bind marker); the bout uses the bound state.
- **R-43** Merging a placed part replaces it in place if the footprint fits; otherwise the merged part goes to the bench.
- **R-44** A limb driven by two branches uses the faster branch's speed and the sum of torques (capped at 2× the faster branch's torque).
- **R-45** If both builds' mainsprings are destroyed on the same tick, the higher remaining HP fraction wins (R-26 tie rule applies).

### 4.9 Invariants

- **INV-1** Exactly one mainspring per build at every point.
- **INV-2** Power solution is unique for a given chassis (solver is order-independent; test by shuffling part order).
- **INV-3** No part's speed exceeds `S₀ × max_ratio_product` from its path; torque never negative.
- **INV-4** Bout outcome is identical when replayed from `(buildA, buildB, seed)` on any platform.
- **INV-5** Coins never negative; bench ≤ capacity; parts never overlap.
- **INV-6** A destroyed part never acts after its destruction tick.
- **INV-7** Swapping sides (mirror) with mirrored RNG mapping yields the mirrored result.

## 7. AI Approach

There is no live opponent. AI provides build bots (to generate the ghost pool and run balance sims) and the ghost matcher.

| Component | Algorithm | Budget |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `BuildBot.novice` | Buys the cheapest affordable part with archetype affinity; places it in the first legal cell adjacent to a driven part | 5 ms per round |
| `BuildBot.competent` | Evaluates every shop offer with `PlacementSearch` (try each legal placement and rotation, score by `TrainReport` expected DPS × effective HP), buys greedily within budget | 50 ms per round |
| `BuildBot.strong` | Beam search (width 8) over buy/sell/reroll/merge sequences for the round; scores candidate builds by simulating 6 bouts vs sampled ghosts of the next round | 2 s per round (offline only, CI and pool generation) |
| `GhostPoolGenerator` | Runs 20,000 bot runs (mix 20% novice, 50% competent, 30% strong across archetypes), snapshots every round, deduplicates by build hash, stratifies by (round, wins, losses) | Offline, CI job ≤ 45 min |
| `GhostMatcher` | R-33 bucket draw with the run RNG | ≤ 5 ms |

**Evaluation features:** expected DPS per limb (damage / interval), stamina seconds (wind / drain per second), effective HP (HP weighted by armor), vulnerability (fraction of DPS lost if the single most exposed gear dies), and counter-coverage (ranged vs melee mix). Bots never read the opponent before buying (they cannot; ghosts are drawn after the workshop).

**Determinism:** bots use `SplitMix64(seed ^ botID)`. Pool generation is reproducible from a pool seed recorded in `Resources/Content/ghosts/manifest.json`.
