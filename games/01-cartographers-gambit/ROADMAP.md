## 1. Summary and Design Pillars

- **Hook:** Two rival mapmakers ink a contested continent, and whatever either of you draws becomes real ground for the armies marching across it.
- **Summary:** Each round, players spend scarce ink to draw terrain strokes (ridges, rivers, forests, and roads) onto a shared vellum. Armies then march by simple deterministic rules across whatever terrain exists. Holding surveyed landmarks earns victory points and more ink. The two factions draw differently: the Surveyor draws short straight strokes and cheap roads, and the Romancer draws sweeping curves and mythical terrain.
- **Pillars:**
  1. **The map is the move.** Every meaningful decision is a stroke; menus never replace drawing.
  2. **Terrain is neutral.** Anything you draw can be used by either side; good players draw terrain the enemy cannot exploit.
  3. **Readable at arm's length.** A glance shows who holds what, where armies will go, and what each stroke costs.
  4. **Deterministic combat.** No dice in combat; uncertainty comes from the opponent's strokes and orders.
  5. **Pencil-native, finger-complete.** Pencil adds pressure and hover; every action is possible with a finger.
- **Aesthetic spec:**
  - Palette: vellum `#F3E9D2`, vellum shadow `#E2D3B1`, iron-gall ink `#3B2A1A`, faded ink `#7A6247`, vermilion border accent `#C8442B`, Surveyor ink `#2E3F5C` (blue-black), Romancer ink `#5C2E46` (oxblood), landmark gold `#B8902F`.
  - Linework: ink strokes with slight bleed and pooling at stroke ends; ridges drawn as hachures; rivers as double lines with flow ticks; forests as stippled tree glyphs; roads as dashed double lines.
  - Armies: small engraved figures (banner plus three soldiers) on round bases; strength shown as Roman numerals on the banner.
  - Typography: a humanist serif for headings (bundled open-license font chosen in P6, recorded as an ADR) and system font for numbers in HUD.
  - Motion: ink spreads over 250 ms after a stroke commits; armies march with a stepping bob; captured landmarks are re-inked in the owner's color.
  - Audio: quill scratch tied to stroke speed, paper rustle on camera moves, harpsichord and recorder score in three intensity layers, distant drum on march phase.
  - Haptics: light ticks while drawing proportional to ink spent; a firm thud on combat resolution.
- **Inspirations:** Feral's draw-a-path controls for ROME: Total War on iPad, Bad North's strict terrain vocabulary, Twilight Struggle's tug-of-war tension, and Apple's Pencil hover and squeeze guidance.

## 2. Tech Stack Decision

Per [`_CONVENTIONS.md`](../_shared/_CONVENTIONS.md) §2, plus:
- **Renderer:** SpriteKit scene `MapScene` with three layers: vellum background, ink layer (a single `SKSpriteNode` whose texture is regenerated by a Metal compute pass when strokes change), and unit/landmark nodes. Rationale: hundreds of strokes as `SKShapeNode`s would be slow; one ink texture is cheap to draw at 120 fps.
- **Input pipeline:** Raw touches/Pencil samples (`CGPoint`, force, altitude) are processed in `CGRender/Input`, simplified with Ramer–Douglas–Peucker (epsilon 0.35 cell), and quantized into an integer `StrokeInput` (list of grid cells plus width 1–3). Only `StrokeInput` enters `CGCore`, which keeps rules deterministic.
- **Turn structure:** Turn-based; no simulation tick in Core. March animation is driven by `Event` playback.
- ADRs to create in P0: ADR-0003 (ink texture via Metal compute), ADR-0004 (grid size 48×32 square cells with 8-connectivity), ADR-0005 (stroke quantization in Render, rasterization in Core).

## 4. Core Rules Specification

**Setup**
- R-1 The board is a 48×32 grid of cells; every cell starts as `plain`, elevation 0.
- R-2 Map generation (seeded) places 2 capitals (one per faction, on opposite thirds) and 7 neutral landmarks at least 6 cells apart and at least 8 cells from either capital, using rejection sampling with the match seed.
- R-3 Each faction starts with 3 armies of strength 4 adjacent to its capital, 20 ink, and 0 victory points (VP).
- R-4 Nib draft: 8 nibs are drawn from the faction-legal pool; players alternate picks starting with the second player (snake order 1-2-2-1-1-2-2-1 starting with player 2), 4 nibs each. Each nib can be used once per match unless its `uses` says otherwise.

**Round Structure (12 rounds maximum)**
- R-5 Each round has phases in order: Income → Draw (Player A) → Draw (Player B) → Orders (both, hidden, simultaneous) → March → Combat → Supply → Scoring → Victory check. First player alternates each round.
- R-6 Income: +3 ink, +2 ink per landmark held, +1 ink if the capital is uncontested. Ink caps at 40.

**Drawing**
- R-7 A stroke is `StrokeInput { terrain, cells: [Cell], width: 1...3 }`; cells must be 8-connected and in order; length (cell count before width expansion) must be 2–12 (Surveyor road strokes up to 18).
- R-8 Width expands each centerline cell to a disk of radius `width-1` (Chebyshev). For `ridge`, width also sets elevation (1–3) on centerline cells and elevation−1 (min 1) on expanded cells.
- R-9 Ink cost = sum over affected cells of `terrain.inkPerCell` (+ faction modifiers + nib modifiers), rounded up; a stroke that costs more than current ink is illegal.
- R-10 Strokes may not touch any cell within Chebyshev distance 1 of an enemy army, any capital, or any landmark cell.
- R-11 Overdrawing replaces the prior terrain in affected cells; ridge elevation takes the maximum of old and new.
- R-12 A player may draw any number of strokes in their Draw phase until they pass or run out of ink. Each stroke is a separate action (undoable within the same phase only, refunding full ink).
- R-13 Erase action: select up to 12 cells; cost = 2 × the ink value of the terrain erased; erased cells revert to `plain` elevation 0; cells occupied by armies cannot be erased.
- R-14 Surveyor drawing restriction: strokes must be composed of at most 2 straight segments (horizontal, vertical, or 45° diagonal). Surveyor road ink cost is halved.
- R-15 Romancer drawing: any curve; standard terrain costs +1 ink per stroke (not per cell); may draw myth terrain (R-24).

**Orders and Movement**
- R-16 Orders: each army receives one order: `hold`, `march(to: Cell)`, or `support(armyID)`. Orders are committed hidden and revealed simultaneously.
- R-17 Movement points: 12 per round. Cost per entered cell (in half-units ×2 to stay integer): plain 2, road 1, forest 4, ridge uphill `2 + 2 × elevationGain`, ridge downhill 2, ford 6, river 0-passable only by bridge (bridge cost 2), myth terrain per R-24.
- R-18 Paths use A* over 8-connectivity; diagonal moves cost the same as orthogonal; ties broken by lowest cumulative cost, then lowest `y`, then lowest `x`.
- R-19 Movement resolves in initiative order: armies sorted by (owner = first player this round first, then army ID). Each army moves step by step along its path; it stops when entering a cell adjacent to an enemy army (engagement) or when out of points.
- R-20 Two armies may not share a cell; a blocked step ends the army's movement.

**Combat**
- R-21 After March, every pair of adjacent enemy armies is engaged. For each engagement (resolved in a deterministic order: lowest attacker ID first), compute `attack = strength + supportBonus − riverPenalty` and `defense = strength + supportBonus + elevationBonus + forestBonus`. The attacker is the army that moved this round (if both moved, the one with higher initiative; if neither moved, no combat).
- R-22 Modifiers: support +1 per supporting friendly army adjacent to the target; elevation bonus +1 per elevation level the defender sits above the attacker; forest +1 if the defender is in forest; river penalty −2 if the attacker's last step crossed a ford or bridge.
- R-23 Resolution: if attack > defense, the defender loses `attack − defense` strength (min 1) and retreats 1 cell away from the attacker (if it cannot retreat it loses 1 more); if defense ≥ attack, the attacker loses `max(1, defense − attack)` strength. An army at strength 0 is destroyed.

**Myth Terrain (Romancer Only)**
- R-24 `serpent_sea`: impassable to Surveyor armies, costs 3 ink per cell, Romancer armies treat it as plain. `enchanted_wood`: costs 2 ink per cell; enemy armies entering lose 1 strength once per round. `giants_causeway`: costs 3 ink per cell; acts as a bridge across rivers for both sides but movement cost 1 for Romancer.

**Supply, Scoring, and Victory**
- R-25 Supply: an army is supplied if a path of movement cost ≤ 20 exists to its capital or a held landmark that avoids rivers (bridges and fords allowed) and enemy-adjacent cells. Unsupplied armies lose 1 strength in the Supply phase.
- R-26 Landmark control: a landmark is held by the faction with the only army within Chebyshev distance 1 at Scoring; contested landmarks keep their previous owner.
- R-27 Scoring: +1 VP per landmark held; +2 VP one-time for first capture of each landmark.
- R-28 Reinforcement: at Scoring, each faction with fewer than 3 armies places a new strength-3 army adjacent to its capital if any adjacent cell is free.
- R-29 Victory: first to 10 VP at a Victory check wins; capturing the enemy capital (an army ends Scoring on the capital cell) wins immediately; after round 12, most VP wins, tie broken by total army strength, then by ink remaining, then the second player wins.

**Invariants (property tests)**
- INV-1 Ink is always within 0…40 for both factions.
- INV-2 No two armies share a cell; no army stands on `serpent_sea` unless it is Romancer.
- INV-3 Every terrain cell has elevation 0 unless its terrain is `ridge` (elevation 1…3).
- INV-4 Total VP never decreases.
- INV-5 Applying the same `Replay` twice yields identical state hashes.
- INV-6 `legalActions(state)` never contains an action the reducer rejects, and the reducer never accepts an action not in `legalActions` (checked on sampled actions).

## 7. AI Approach

- **Candidate stroke generator** (`CandidateStrokes.swift`): produces ≤ 60 candidate strokes per decision from templates: (a) river wall between an enemy army and its likely target, (b) road from own army to nearest unheld landmark, (c) ridge in front of a defending army, (d) forest on a held landmark's approach, (e) erase enemy road segment on the shortest path to our capital, (f) myth terrain blocks for Romancer. Templates use A* paths and are quantized exactly like human input.
- **Order generator:** for each army: hold, march to each of the 3 nearest landmarks, march to the enemy capital, or support the most-threatened ally.
- **Evaluator:** linear features with weights in `ai_weights.json`: VP difference, landmarks held, sum of army strength, supply status, ink difference, distance-to-landmarks (own minus enemy), threatened landmarks, capital safety.
- **Easy (Apprentice):** greedy: best single stroke + best orders by evaluator after one simulated March; adds ε-random choice (ε = 0.25).
- **Medium (Journeyman):** one-ply search over up to 3 strokes (beam width 8) and order combinations, simulating March/Combat deterministically against the opponent's predicted greedy orders.
- **Hard (Master Cartographer):** MCTS over macro-actions (stroke bundle + orders) with determinized opponent orders sampled from the Medium policy; UCT c = 1.2; budget 1.5 s on iPad (A16), 3,000 iterations cap; deterministic given seed (MCTS uses its own `SeededRNG` derived from state hash).
- Time budget enforcement via iteration counting, not wall clock, in tests; wall-clock cap only in shipping builds (recorded as an ADR).
- **Daily map:** seed = `hash("CG-daily-" + yyyyMMdd UTC)`; player vs Hard as Surveyor or Romancer (alternating by weekday); score = 100 × VP margin + 10 × (13 − rounds used) on a win.
