# Game Roadmap Template

Every game's roadmap lives at `games/NN-<slug>/ROADMAP.md` and has the sections below, in this order. This is the contract described in "The Game Roadmap Contract" in the program `ROADMAP.md`. A section that is missing or vague is filled in stage G0, marked "(filled in G0)", and never left blank.

Copy everything below the line into a new game's `ROADMAP.md`.

---

# <Working Name>

## Pitch

One paragraph: what the game is, who it is for, and why it is fun.

## Core Loop

What the player does second to second, what a session looks like from start to finish, and what brings them back.

## Controls and Platforms

- **Controls:** the touch gestures, and game-controller support if any.
- **Devices:** iPhone (always), iPad (layout notes), and Mac (only if wanted).
- **Orientation:** portrait, landscape, or both.

## Content Scope for v1.0.0

What ships at launch, with numbers: levels, worlds, modes, characters, items, difficulty settings, and so on. List anything deliberately left for later under "After v1.0.0" at the end.

## Look and Sound

Art style, palette, and mood; music and sound direction; and how assets will be made (original, procedural, or CC0). Note color-blind-safe choices.

## Engine Needs

Which shared engine modules the game uses, and anything it needs that the engine does not have yet.

## Monetization

Paid up front, free, or free with in-app purchases. For each purchase: what it unlocks and the suggested price tier. "None" is a valid answer. No advertising.

## Game-Specific Risks

What is hardest to get right (for example game feel, difficulty balance, performance, or content volume), and how each risk will be tested early.

## Milestones

Ordered milestones mapped onto the program stages, each with a **Done when** line that a command or a recorded manual step can check.

### M1: Core Loop (stage G2)

**Done when:** …

### M2: Content (stage G3)

**Done when:** …

### M3: Polish (stage G4)

**Done when:** …

### M4: Quality Assurance (stage G5)

**Done when:** …

## Done When (v1.0.0)

The game-level definition of done, on top of the program's Per-Game Launch Gate (stage G6).

## After v1.0.0

Ideas and cut features for later versions.
